## Priorities without scope loss

The next useful implementation slices are native selected-file access, a measured Rust engine experiment, a real read-only Flipper adapter and completion of the REA engine workflow. Their ordering can change with available devices and toolchains. That does not delete later radio, vehicle, industrial, Wi-Fi, mesh or specialist recovery requirements.

The physical acceptance and licence gates are delivery work, not reasons to rebrand APEX as a permanently offline-only application. Conversely, preserving the broad vision does not license reporting proposed capability as completed.
